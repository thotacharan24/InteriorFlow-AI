# Business Impact and ROI

## Assumptions
Monthly lead volume, hourly cost and conversion lift are pilot targets, not measured claims.

## Illustrative scenarios

| Scenario | Hours saved/month | Gross value at INR 300/hour | Conservative operating caveat |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Conservative | 8 | 2,400 | 5-minute lead/task savings over 100 leads |
| Moderate | 13.3 | 3,990 | 100 leads plus marketing drafts |
| Optimistic | 30 | 9,000 | Pilot with broad follow-up and coordination savings |

Subtract Gemini/API, n8n hosting, maintenance and staff-review costs. Measure baseline and pilot response time, follow-up coverage, lead conversion and project overdue count before evaluating return.
