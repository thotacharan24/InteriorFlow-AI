# Interior Design Industry Research Summary

This summary separates public, non-proprietary findings from internal assumptions. It does not claim to be a complete market survey.

| Challenge | Evidence basis | Business impact | Automation | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missed enquiries | Public inquiry channels generally require fast human response; no firm-specific conversion data is assumed | Lost qualified demand | Central webhook and acknowledgement | Capture rate, first response time |
| Manual lead qualification | Enquiry information is often fragmented across calls and forms | Slow routing | Explainable lead score | Qualification minutes |
| Inconsistent follow-up | Manual follow-up depends on staff availability | Lower follow-up coverage | Due-task sequence with consent | Due/completed tasks |
| Low marketing consistency | Social media requires repeated content production | Uneven reach | AI drafts requiring approval | Drafts/week |
| Scope and revision churn | Interior design involves changing customer preferences and selections | Rework and delays | Requirement capture and approval milestones | Revision count |
| Vendor coordination | Material and labour dependencies can be cross-functional | Schedule risk | Milestone alerts and escalation | Overdue milestones |
| Payment collection | Deferred milestone amounts require tracking | Cash-flow risk | Invoice/due-date reminders | Days sales outstanding |
| Fragmented customer communication | Requests arrive across WhatsApp, calls and email | Incomplete history | Central activity log | Resolution time |

Verified starting sources:
- Houzz Research: https://www.houzz.com/research
- Government of India, IBEF construction overview: https://www.ibef.org/industry/construction-presentation
- Government of India, NSDC skill ecosystem: https://nsdcindia.org/

No invented statistics are used. Pilot targets should be measured before and after implementation.
